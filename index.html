What Stays After The Camera Stops.

JAGNAAM PICTURES is an independent film production studio rooted in the soil of Karnataka, built around stories that stay with you long after the frame ends.

We believe cinema doesn't need to say everything. Our stories favour restraint, atmosphere, character and lived-in worlds — letting silence, place and performance speak for themselves.

From development and writing through production, direction and post, we carry every story under one creative vision.

MANJU S PADMANABH

The Founder of Jagnaam Pictures

The founder of JAGNAAM PICTURES, Manju S Padmanabh has spent nearly a decade working across the Kannada film industry — writing, directing, producing, editing and colouring films from within the process itself.

His experience across departments shapes the way JAGNAAM approaches filmmaking: from developing a story and building its world to production and the final image, every stage remains connected to the same creative intent.

Those Three Years (Working Title)

In Pre-Production
Proof of Concept Film

Set in rural Karnataka in 1980, Those Three Years is a proof-of-concept film built as the first chapter of an original feature story. Two lives born under the same roof grow together for twenty-three years — until, on the day meant to unite them forever, one man places the woman he loves into another man's hands. Restrained, intimate and deliberately withholding, the film ends where its title begins — leaving three unseen years at the heart of the feature yet to come.
